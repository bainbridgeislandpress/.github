## About

**Bainbridge Island Press** is an independent poetry publishing house on Bainbridge Island in Washington’s Puget Sound, founded Christmas Day 2022. We are a traditional press with a simple mission: give more poets a path to print — especially first books — and put beautiful work into the hands of readers.

We love the talent of Bainbridge Island and the Olympic Peninsula, and we publish authors from around the world.

> *The future of poetry is bright. We’re building a more accessible world for writers and readers alike.*

## What we publish

| Line | What it is | Explore |
|:-----|:-----------|:--------|
| **Full-length collections** | Book manuscripts for poets ready for a complete volume | [Submit](https://bainbridgeislandpress.submittable.com/submit) · [Titles](https://bainbridgeisland.press/collections/all) |
| **XPRESS chapbooks** | Short, fast, zine-spirited chapbooks (20–40 pages) that get work into readers’ hands quickly | [XPRESS](https://bainbridgeisland.press/collections/xpress-chapbooks) · [Submit](https://bainbridgeislandpress.submittable.com/submit) |
| ***POETICS* journal** | Semi-annual journal & podcast exploring poetry from the press and beyond | [POETICS](https://bainbridgeisland.press) · [Substack](https://bainbridgeislandpress.substack.com) |

No reading fees for manuscript or chapbook submissions. Published book authors are compensated.
